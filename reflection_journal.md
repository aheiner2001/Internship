# 📓 Reflection Journal

A personal log of thoughts, experiences, and reflections from the internship.
Entries are lightly edited for spelling and grammar — your voice is kept intact.

---

## How to Use
- Paste your rough notes under a new `## Entry` heading with the date.
- The agent will clean up spelling, grammar, and formatting while preserving your words.
- Entries are also cross-linked to the milestone folder they fall in.
